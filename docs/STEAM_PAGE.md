## Short description (256 / 300 characters)

One player watches the CCTV. The rest hunt the crowd blind, going only on what they hear over the radio. The fugitive keeps changing clothes, faking a limp and listening in. A 4-5 player party game about describing people badly and arresting the wrong guy.

## About this game

**Somewhere in this crowd is the person you're looking for. You just can't see them.**

A busy town festival at sunset. Hundreds of people, food stalls, a concert, a ferris wheel. One of them is a fugitive with a job to do.

**The Watcher** sits in the security booth with a wall of grainy black-and-white cameras and a witness sketch that's only half right. Find the fugitive on screen, then describe them over the radio: *"Tall. Hunched. Long dark coat. Limping on the left. Heading past the mask stall."*

**The field team** is down in the crowd and sees none of it. The Patrol carries the only three pairs of handcuffs. The Plainclothes officer questions witnesses and checks the changing tents for missing clothes. The police Dog sniffs out whatever the fugitive left behind.

**The fugitive** blends in. Swap your coat in a changing tent. Put on a fox mask. Fake a limp for a few seconds. Do your three jobs without anyone noticing, and get out before the festival closes. And stand close enough to the police, because their radio is not as private as they think.

### Features

- **Describe, don't point.** The Watcher can't mark anyone on the map. Everything goes through your voice, your words and your sense of left and right.
- **Descriptions go stale.** Every outfit change makes the last report wrong. Old clothes left in a tent tell the police what to look for next.
- **Everyone walks differently.** Limps, slouches, bounces. Rookies watch clothes. Veterans watch walks.
- **Three cuffs, no take-backs.** Arrest the wrong person and the cuff is gone, the fugitive gets more time and the crowd scatters.
- **A festival that runs on a schedule.** The concert pulls the crowd to the stage, fireworks blind the cameras, and the sun goes down while you play.
- **Watch it back.** Every round ends with a replay of everything you missed, usually the fugitive strolling right past you.
- 4-5 players online. Play with friends or in public lobbies.
